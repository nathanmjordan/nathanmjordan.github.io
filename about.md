I was born and raised in rural south Alabama. I'm a first-generation university student and attended Auburn University at Montgomery in the state's capital. I earned my bachelor of science degree in political science with minors in history and economics in 2024.
At AUM, I was fortunate to be offered research assistant positions with multiple faculty members in the Department of Political Science and Public Administration. During this time, I became passionate about social scientific research as a vocation.

After earning my degree, I began graduate studies at the University of Georgia. I've worked as a research assistant to professors Trey Hood and David Cotrell, and as a teaching assistant to Charles Bullock and Jeffrey Glas. I've taken methodological training in text as data and time and space regression modeling. I've also presented research at multiple conferences including annual meetings of the Southern Political Science Association and Midwest Political Science Association.
I was also fortunate to take a graduate summer course at the University of Munich's American Studies Institute in 2026.

In my free time I enjoy live music (electronic and metal), gaming (adventure, immersive sims, role-playing games), museums, astronomy, world history, model collecting, retro computing, and travel.
